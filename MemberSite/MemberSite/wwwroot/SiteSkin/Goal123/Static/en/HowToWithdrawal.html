HOW TO WITHDRAW

Step 1: After logging into Goal123’s website. Click on "CASHIER" and choose "Withdraw".

Step 2: Update bank information at "My Account"

  • If you haven’t updated your information on the Profile page, please update first.
  • You can add multiple bank accounts at the same time.
  • Your real name, bank account name, and your ID name have to be the same.

After filling in all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button.

 

Step 3: Click the button Add a bank account and fill in the information

Step 4: Choose your preferred bank account, input the amount (min. 50,000VND), and click the button Submit.

We will verify and notify you as soon as possible.

You can check the status of your withdrawal order at "Withdrawal history"

Note:

  1. Goal123 requests to verify your personal identity details (KYC) on the first withdrawal. The required documents may include the following ones.
    • Original photo of Identity card
    • Original photo of Passport
    • Original photo of Driving license
  2. You can only withdraw money with the same currency that you registered your account with.
  3. All withdrawal requests are only done with registered names and details.
  4. You have to bet at least 100% deposit amount before submitting the request. All rejected, void and tie bets will not be counted as valid turnover. If you don’t meet this requirement, a 20% fee will be applied upon your withdrawal.
  5. We reserve the right to modify the payment method for your withdrawal (fund out) request at our sole and absolute discretion. We also reserve the right to deduct part or all of the remittance costs incurred in such request. The Company will notify users of its decision via email. The Company's decision is final.
