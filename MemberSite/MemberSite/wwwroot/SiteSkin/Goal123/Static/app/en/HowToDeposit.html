How To Deposit in Goal123

Step 1: “LOG IN” your account

Step 2: Fill in the username and password entries then click “Log in”

Step 3: Click “Deposit”

Step 4: We offer 3 payment methods as below

  1. ONLINE BANKING

Step 1: In this tab, please select one option among EeziePay, Help2Pay, VNPay, CirclePay.

Step 2: Chose one bank you have.

Step 3: Fill in the amount of money (there will be ,000 in default. If you want to make a deposit of 300,000 VND, then you need to type 300). The minimum amount is 300,000 (300) – maximum amount is 25,000,000 (25,000).

Step 4: Check your information and click to confirm.

Step 5: Website will be direct to payment page. Please log in your bank account and proceed payment.

Step 6: After you complete your deposit, the balance in general wallet will be updated.

Step 7: Scroll down and you will see the status of your latest deposit.

  1. LOCAL BANK TRANSFER

Step 1: Select “Local Bank Transfer”

Step 2: Chose bank and click “Send” to complete your transaction (there will be ,000 in default. If you want to make a deposit of 300,000 VND, then you need to type 300).

Step 3: Check your information and click “confirm”.

Step 4: Scroll down and you will see the status of your latest deposit.

  1. MOBILE E-WALLET

Step 1: You can choose one between Zalo Pay or Viettel Pay.

Step 2: Select one wallet and type the deposit amount. Then click “Send” to complete transaction (there will be ,000 in default. If you want to make a deposit of 300,000 VND, then you need to type 300).

Step 3: Check your information and click “confirm”.

Step 4: Website will be direct to payment page. Please scan QR code to complete your transaction.

Step 5: Scroll down and you will see the status of your latest deposit.
