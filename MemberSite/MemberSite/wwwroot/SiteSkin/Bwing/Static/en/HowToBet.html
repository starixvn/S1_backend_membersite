HOW TO BET

Step 1: Register a Bwing account

  • Access: https://www.bwing.com/
  • Select "Register" and fill in information including: Username, password, Email, choose currency in VND.
  • Check the box “I agree to the Bwing Terms and Conditions and the Bwing Rules and Regulations. I confirm I am over 18 years old”
  • Click "COMPLETE REGISTRATION"

Step 2: Sign in and top up

Log in to your Bwing account and choose any deposit method to deposit into your Bwing account. Your balance will appear at the top of the screen as shown.

See also: How to send money Bwing (hyperlink)

Step 3: Transfer funds and Choose your favorite game

Bwing account has two (2) wallets:

Main wallet: participating in Bwing CASINO products; LIVE CASINO and FISH WORLD

Sportsbook wallet: participating in products in SPORTS, VIRTUAL SPORTS, ESPORTS, SABA CLUB, OW CASINO, and LOTTERY.

Go to "Cashier", choose "Transfer Funds", choose your wallet and enter the amount you want to transfer. Then, press the button "Transfer" to complete.

Step 4: Place a bet

Successful fund transfer balance will be available in main wallet. You can start choosing your favorite game and place your bets. Bwing offers a wide range of games from sports, casino to slots or fishing games. Bets with competitive odds for tournaments in market. Start to experience now!
