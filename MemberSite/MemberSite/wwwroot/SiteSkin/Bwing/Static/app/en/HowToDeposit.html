HOW TO DEPOSIT

Step 1: "LOGIN" at the homepage

Step 2: Fill in the registered information and click "LOGIN" button.

Step 3: Click "Cashier" and choose "Deposit".

Step 4: Choose your preferred deposit method

1. Online banking

Step 1: Choose one of the appropriate payment gateways: CirclePay, Help2pay, VNPAY, EaziePay.

Step 2: Select the bank you want to use and enter the amount to be transacted (by default system has 3 zero at the end. If a member wants to deposit 300,000, just enter 300)

Step 3: Login to your online banking to make a deposit.

Step 4: After sending money successfully, the main wallet at Bwing will be credited

Step 5: Check your deposit status at “Recent transactions” at the bottom of the Deposit page

2. Local bank transfer

Step 1: Choose Local bank transfer

Step 2: Choose 1 of the available banks. Enter the amount to deposit and click "Submit" to complete the transaction. The system defaults to 3 zeros at the end. If a member wants to deposit 300,000 just enter 300

Step 3: Check the information and click “Submit” again

Step 4: Get the bank information that appears at the pop up window. Then fill in the transaction information, click "Choose file" to upload the photo of the receipt/bank statement (if any), the reference number from bank slip is the reference number. Click “Confirm” to alert Bwing to process the transaction.

Step 5: Check your deposit status at “Recent transactions” at the bottom of the Deposit page

3. Mobile E-wallet

Step 1: Select e-wallets Momo, Zalo Pay or Viettel Pay.

Step 2: Click on the selected e-wallet again and choose the amount. Click “Confirm” to start the transaction

Step 3: Fill in the required password when making transactions for Momo and Viettel Pay e-wallets. As for Zalo Pay, please scan the code at the App Zalo Pay.

Step 4: Check your deposit status at “Recent transactions” at the bottom of the Deposit page
